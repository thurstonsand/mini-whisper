# Menu bar styling: one visual language for the dropdown

The status menu has grown by accretion: native menu items, the status line, the quick-add accordion with its own hosted-view styling, and repair rows all render with their own decisions about weight, color, spacing, and iconography. The result is visibly inconsistent.

Walk the whole dropdown as one surface and settle a single visual language — typography, semantic colors, spacing, icon usage, hover/selection treatment — for native and hosted items alike. The quick-add spike's pixel-parity laws (ticket 34) are the precedent for making hosted views indistinguishable from native rows; extend that bar to everything in the menu.
